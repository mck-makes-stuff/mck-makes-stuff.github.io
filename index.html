Hi, I’m

Gail McKinney

I'm a math and computer science student at Yale University. I work on AI assurance, a nebulous but imperative undertaking that involves rigorous testing to assess how safe, capable, and aligned AI systems are.

I spent two internships at the Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory building evaluation methods for operational and healthcare AI. The work I like best has to do with math, human-centered computing, linguistics, and public health. My work is interdisciplinary because my grounding force is service of humanity. Humans are not confined to any single discipline.
